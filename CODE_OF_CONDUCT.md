# Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

## Commitment / 承诺

This project welcomes contributors and users regardless of background, experience or identity. Treat people with respect, review the work rather than attacking the person, and preserve privacy. 本项目倡导具体、诚实、可复现的讨论与人工负责的审阅。

## Expected behavior / 行为

- Be considerate; disagree with evidence and describe concrete technical effects.
- Respect personal boundaries and avoid harassment, threats, hate, sexualized targeting or publishing private information.
- Do not post real chat exports, credentials or private Host details. Use sanitized synthetic reproductions.
- Give accurate test and provenance claims; distinguish unrun, failed and passed checks.
- AI-generated contributions remain the human contributor's responsibility. AI/bots do not grant their own approvals or merge.

## Reporting and enforcement / 报告与处理

Report concerns privately to a human maintainer through a contact they actually publish. If no private channel is available, request one without exposing affected people or private material. Security issues follow [SECURITY](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may remove harmful content, require changes, restrict participation or suspend access, based on context and severity. They should explain actions proportionately and protect reporter privacy. Review disputes involving a maintainer should be handled by another available human maintainer. No automated AI judgment is final enforcement.

## Scope / 适用

Applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, project-managed discussions and conduct while representing this project. It does not claim authority over unrelated private spaces. Questions and improvement proposals belong in a focused issue that protects personal information.
